Marquee

Marquee is a personal, non-commercial media app built by Rafael Hernandez Garcia for his own phone, TV and computers. It is not sold, advertised or distributed to the public.

Signing in with Google lets Marquee keep watch progress, My List and preferences the same on every device signed in to the same account. That data is stored in a hidden app folder in the user's own Google Drive.

Movie and show information comes from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
