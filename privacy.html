MARQUEE

Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Marquee is a personal app used by its developer. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What Marquee accesses

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account is signed in.
  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). Marquee can read and write only the files it creates there. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

What is stored, and where

In that app-data folder Marquee keeps your watch progress (which titles, how far), My List, saved filters and subtitle-language choices, so they sync between your devices. The same information is also stored on each of your devices. Nothing is stored on any server operated by Marquee: there is none.

What is never done

  • Your data is not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising.
  • No person reads your data. It is used only to sync your own devices.
  • Marquee does not use your Google data to train or improve any AI or machine-learning model.

Marquee's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out in Marquee's Settings to stop syncing. To delete the synced data, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Marquee and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove Marquee's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Marquee.
